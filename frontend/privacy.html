Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Opara Joel, an individual operator ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use Music Intelligence Engine (the "Service"), available at the web address where you are reading this page.

1. Information we collect

We keep data collection to what the Service actually needs to operate.

  • Sign-in details from Google. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, your email address, and your Google account identifier, together with basic profile information made available by the OpenID Connect scopes openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile. This sign-in step requests no access to your Gmail mailbox.
  • Gmail send authorisation (only if you connect it). Connecting Gmail is a separate, optional step that is not part of Google sign-in. If you choose to connect it, the Service requests only the gmail.send permission and stores the resulting OAuth credentials so that it can send outreach email from your own Gmail account on your behalf. We do not request or receive access to read your inbox, your messages, or any other Gmail data.
  • Activity and content you create. Information you enter or generate in the Service, such as your music discovery activity (radio stations and DJs you search for, save, or open), selected recipients, outreach records and their status history, message drafts, release and track details, and any notes you add.
  • Data stored in your browser. Your session token and a small sender preference are stored locally in your browser (local storage / session storage) so you stay signed in and the Service remembers your choice of sending address. This data stays in your browser and is not a tracking cookie.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your personal information. We do not read your Gmail. The Service does not ask for or store payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
  • To provide the Service — station and DJ discovery and intelligence, opportunity matching, and outreach record keeping.
  • To send outreach email from your connected Gmail account, and only when you explicitly ask the Service to send.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the Service and to fix problems.

4. Music and contact discovery

The Service discovers radio stations, DJs, and public contact routes by searching publicly available web pages. Discovery searches use station, DJ, genre, and location terms rather than your personal account data. Contact details and other facts shown in the Service come from public sources and may be presented with confidence estimates and source attribution; they are not guaranteed to be accurate, current, or complete.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only with the providers needed to run the Service, and only for the purposes described here:

  • Google — sign-in (Google Identity) and, if you connect it, sending email through the Gmail API under your own account.
  • Railway — application and database hosting for the Service backend.
  • Vercel — hosting of this website.
  • SerpAPI — search of publicly available web pages used to discover station and DJ information. These searches use discovery terms and public sources, not your personal account data.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Service and its users.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses essential local storage in your browser to keep you signed in and to remember your sending preference. It does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking technologies.

7. Data retention

We retain your account information and the content you create for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you disconnect Gmail, the stored Gmail send credentials are removed. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data as described in section 9.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from within the Service, which stops future sending from your account and removes the stored Gmail send credentials.
  • You can update your display name in your profile.
  • To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated data, email us at oparajoel@gmail.com. Please include the email address associated with your account. There is currently no automated in-app account-deletion control, so requests are handled by the operator. We will respond to your request.

The rights available to you may depend on where you live. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children. It is not available to anyone under the age of 18.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. We take steps required by applicable law to protect information when it is transferred.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date shown above. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.

13. Contact

For privacy requests, support, or any question about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact the operator at oparajoel@gmail.com.
